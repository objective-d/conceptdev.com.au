Foodie

Effortless recipe management

The simple and powerful way to manage and share your favourite recipes. With a clean, photo-based interface and a range of kitchen tools, it's also the perfect cooking companion.

Your recipes. Your way.

Manage

Effortlessly manage your recipes with a simple and intuitive interface. Add your own, or import them from any website with a couple of taps.

Organise

Quickly search for, and group recipes into collections that are meaningful to you – forget the one-size-fits-all approach.

Sync

iCloud syncing keeps your recipes up to date across all your devices.

Your cooking companion

Recipe scaling

Scale recipes up or down by simply changing the serving size – the ingredients scale automatically. Switch between metric and imperial with a single tap.

Cooking tools

Easily convert between common units. Use the kitchen timer with prep and cook times predefined for each recipe.

Kitchen friendly

Big & bright directions help you focus on your cooking. Let Foodie read your recipe aloud using hands-free mode.

Share with family and friends

Share

Share your recipes with a single tap – by AirDrop, email or PDF, or print them. Share an entire collection with other Foodie users and everyone's collections stay in sync – perfect for keeping a shared cookbook with your family and friends.

Shopping lists

Add a recipe's ingredients to a shopping list, grouped by aisle. Shared lists update live for everyone, or share them through the Reminders app.

Meal plan

Plan the week, and keep it in a shared Calendar for everyone to see.
