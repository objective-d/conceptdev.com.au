Privacy Policy — Stiction

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Stiction does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

Stiction remembers how far you have got: which levels you have cleared, your best score and fewest shots on each one, the difficulty you are playing at, whether sound and music are on, and whether you have unlocked the full game.

All of that is stored in the app's own preferences on your device. It never leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Stiction makes no network requests

The game itself never connects to anything. There is no server behind it, and nothing to send. It plays exactly the same with the device in aeroplane mode.

Two of Apple's own services can use the network on your instruction, and only then.

Unlocking the full game

If you buy the unlock, or restore a previous purchase, the App Store handles it. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card details, your Apple Account, or your name. The app is told one thing in return — whether the unlock is owned — and stores that single yes or no on your device.

If you never buy anything, no purchase request is ever made.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and off unless you are signed in to it on your device.

When you are signed in, Stiction submits your scores to Apple's leaderboards so they can be ranked against other players. What Apple receives is the score and the Game Center identity you already chose — nothing else about you, and nothing else from the app. Apple's own privacy policy governs it: apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is submitted and the game is unaffected.

What Stiction never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No analytics or crash reporting services
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data
  • No data sold or shared with anyone, because none is collected

Children

Stiction is safe for all ages. Because it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date at the top will change.
