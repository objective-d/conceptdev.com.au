Purchases, restoring and refunds

This is the same for every app we make.

I paid, but the app still thinks I have not

Find Restore Purchase in the app and tap it. That asks the App Store again and re-applies what you own. It works on any device signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

If it reports nothing to restore, you are almost certainly signed in to a different Apple Account from the one that bought it.

Do I have to pay again on my other device?

No. Purchases follow your Apple Account. Install the app, tap Restore Purchase, and it is yours again.

The store says it is unavailable

The App Store could not be reached or is not answering. It is almost always temporary — check your connection and try again shortly. Nothing is charged.

Refunds

Apple takes the payment, so Apple issues refunds — we are not able to. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose what I have if I delete an app?

Anything saved on the device goes with it. Purchases can always be restored; progress and settings cannot, unless the app says otherwise.

Stiction

How do I play?

Tap to fire. That is the whole control scheme — the rest is meant to be worked out. There is a How to Play screen in Settings if you would rather be told.

A level will not open

Levels open in order: clear the one before it. Clearing means reaching that level's target, which is printed on its card.

I cleared a level, but it is locked again on a harder setting

Deliberate. Progress is kept per difficulty. Clearing a level opens the next one at that setting and every easier setting — but not at a harder one, or the difficulty dial would be a way to skip the game.

It does work downwards: finish something on a hard setting and it stays open if you drop back down.

My scores disappeared when I moved the difficulty

They have not. Best scores are kept per difficulty too, so each setting keeps its own records. Move the dial back and they are where you left them.

Cheat mode is not saving my scores

Correct, and intended. With every level already open, a run in cheat mode cannot set a record or unlock anything. Switch it off in Settings and scores count again.

I cannot see the leaderboards

Leaderboards are Game Center, so you need to be signed in to it — Settings › Game Center on your device. The button stays hidden while you are signed out rather than showing something that cannot work. Scores set in cheat mode are never submitted.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Progress is stored on the device only. Your purchase follows your Apple Account; your scores and cleared levels do not travel with it.

Conversion

Do I need a connection?

Only for currency. Every other category works entirely offline.

My exchange rates look out of date

Rates are downloaded when you open the Currency category and cached so conversions keep working without a connection. If the cached rates are stale, open Currency with a connection available and they will refresh.

Rates are published third-party figures for reference. They are not a live dealing rate, and they will not match exactly what a bank or a card charges you.

It opens on the wrong units

Conversion deliberately opens where you left it — the last category and pair of units you used. Change them once and that becomes the new starting point.
